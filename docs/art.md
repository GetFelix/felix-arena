# Art direction

Felix Arena is low-poly sci-fi under a night sky, and [the style frame](../prototype/style-frame.html) is the reference for all of it. The palette is a deep blue-slate world (void `#0a0f15`, floor panels `#2a3646` and `#2e3b4d`, walls `#2b3544`) with off-white hulls and steel-grey props, one Felix cyan accent (`hsl(192, 90%, 42%)`, glowing at `hsl(190, 95%, 58%)`) used only for light: wall trim, floor markings and the centre crystal, never for a team or a prop. The two teams are Coral (`hsl(12, 90%, 60%)`) and Violet (`hsl(262, 75%, 64%)`), which stay apart from each other and from cyan for colour-blind players too. Lighting is one warm key that casts every shadow, a cool hemisphere fill, a cyan rim from behind so silhouettes lift off the floor, and an environment map from a Poly Haven studio HDRI for soft reflections. Materials are matte to satin standard materials with low metalness; nothing is chrome and nothing is textured except the floor, which is drawn on a canvas. The camera looks down at about 55 degrees with a narrow 30-degree lens, so the arena reads like a board, and the kill cam may drop lower and orbit. Effects are additive and bright enough to bloom: team-coloured bolts with a soft halo, a four-point muzzle flare, sparks, a ground ring and a little debris on a hit; post processing is ambient occlusion, bloom on emissive surfaces only, Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapping (it keeps the palette's hues) and a light vignette. Models come from Kenney's Space Kit (CC0), with credits in [prototype/assets/CREDITS.md](../prototype/assets/CREDITS.md). Four rules keep it consistent as content grows: every colour comes from the palette module and nowhere else; every model is restyled by its material names (`metal`, `metalDark`, `dark`, `metalRed`, `rock`, `crystal`) so a Kenney model never shows its own orange; new models come from the same Kenney family or are made to match its proportions and flat shading; and anything that glows is either cyan scenery or a team colour, so light always means something.
